Personal integration

OpenCode Google Tools

A personal OpenCode integration for Google Workspace and Google Apps Script. It lets the OpenCode agent act on your Google data—with your consent—through Google's official OAuth flow and APIs.

What it does

  • Create and update Google Apps Script projects and manage script content.
  • Read and update Google Sheets data and manage spreadsheets.
  • Read and update Google Docs and manage Drive files and folders.
  • Search and send Gmail or manage Gmail labels when explicitly requested.
  • View and create Google Calendar events when explicitly requested.

What happens to your data

OpenCode Google Tools does not operate a central server. Requests go from your machine to Google's APIs through OAuth. Your credentials and refresh token are stored locally on your computer.

The tool accesses your data only when you explicitly ask it to perform an action. It is not used to sell data or send data to third parties.

Important

This is a personal-use tool. Google may display an “unverified app” warning because it requests sensitive scopes such as Gmail and Drive. You control access through your Google Account; you can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Using these APIs is subject to Google's API Terms and Google APIs Terms of Service.
